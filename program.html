What Students Will Do

✉️

Write Professional Emails

Learn to communicate with teachers, mentors, and future employers in a professional tone.

Design with Canva

Create stunning presentations, social media graphics, and personal branding materials.

Practice Public Speaking

Build confidence presenting in front of peers. Learn breathing, posture, and voice projection.

Develop an Elevator Pitch

Craft and deliver a compelling 60-second pitch about themselves or an idea.

Meet Guest Speakers

Hear from professionals in STEM, business, law, and entrepreneurship.

Pitch at Showcase

Present final projects at our Shark Tank-style finale event.
