Week by week breakdown. Skills for life.

Public speaking, body language, and confidence.

Week-by-Week Overview

📧
Week 1

Professional Communication

Master professional email writing. Learn tone, structure, subject lines, and how to make a great first impression when reaching out to teachers, mentors, and future employers.

Project: Write emails for real scenarios (asking for a recommendation, inquiring about opportunities).

🎨
Week 2

Digital Branding with Canva

Create stunning visuals and develop your personal brand. Learn design principles, color theory, typography, and how to use Canva like a pro.

Project: Design a personal brand board showcasing your interests and goals.

🎤
Week 3

Public Speaking Basics

Find your voice and own the room. Learn breathing techniques, body language, voice projection, and how to manage nervous energy.

Project: Deliver a 2-minute talk on something you're passionate about.

💼
Week 4

Career Exploration

Discover paths you didn't know existed. Explore different industries, job roles, and what it takes to succeed. Meet guest speakers from various fields.

Project: Research and present on a career that interests you.

🚀
Week 5

Elevator Pitching

Craft your perfect pitch. Learn to communicate your ideas clearly and persuasively in 60 seconds or less. Practice, refine, and polish.

Project: Write and rehearse your personal elevator pitch.

🏆
Week 6

Shark Tank Showcase

Put it all together. Present your elevator pitch to mentors, peers, and guest speakers at our finale celebration event.

Project: Final pitch presentation + certificate ceremony.

Example Project: Elevator Pitch

By the end of the program, every student will have their own polished pitch.

✏️

Write

Draft your pitch: Who are you? What are you passionate about? What makes you unique?

🔄

Revise

Get feedback from mentors and peers. Refine your message. Cut the fluff.

🎯

Deliver

Present your pitch at the showcase with confidence and clarity.
