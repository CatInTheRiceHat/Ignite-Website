# Ignite Website — Image Prompts for Whisk

**Brand colors to reference:** warm orange (#F07B2A), soft cream/peach (#FFF8F0), golden yellow (#FFD166), deep charcoal navy (#1A1A2E)  
**Vibe:** energetic, warm, youthful, inclusive, inspiring — NOT corporate or stock-photo-generic  
**Style tip:** illustrated/graphic style works better than photorealistic for these sections

---

## Background Images (Full-Width Sections)

These go behind text with a light white/cream overlay on top, so they need to be visually interesting but not too cluttered in the center. Make them **wide landscape** (16:9 ratio).

---

### 1. `Hero.png` — Main Landing Page Hero
**Where it's used:** The very first thing visitors see. Full-screen background behind the headline "Free Mentorship Program for Middle School Students."

**Whisk prompt:**
> Wide landscape illustration of a vibrant spark or flame bursting upward at the center, surrounded by floating icons of laptops, lightbulbs, microphones, and stars. Warm orange and golden yellow color palette on a deep charcoal navy background. Energetic and youthful — feels like potential and excitement. Left and right sides should be slightly darker so white text in the center reads clearly. No people, just visual energy and symbolic icons.

---

### 2. `What You'll Get.png` — "Key Benefits" Section
**Where it's used:** Background behind 6 feature cards (Career Skills, Career Exposure, Mentorship, Free, No Experience Needed, Shark Tank Finale).

**Whisk prompt:**
> Wide landscape illustration showing a bright, sunny scene of diverse middle school students (ages 11–14) learning and collaborating — one presenting at a whiteboard, one on a laptop, one talking confidently. Warm cream and orange tones with soft golden light. The left and right edges should fade to white/cream so card content placed over it stays readable. Happy, motivated, genuine energy — not posed or stock-photo-like. Illustrated style, slightly painterly.

---

### 3. `Who is Ignite For.png` — "Who Is Ignite For?" Section
**Where it's used:** Background behind 3 cards: Grades 6–8, Online & Zoom, Curious Minds.

**Whisk prompt:**
> Wide landscape illustration of three diverse middle school students (grades 6–8) shown together — one looking curious with a raised eyebrow, one on a video call waving at a laptop screen, one with a backpack looking excited. Soft warm background in peach and cream tones. Light and airy composition — lots of open sky or soft gradient behind them. Illustrated style with clean lines. Center area should be relatively open for text overlay.

---

### 4. `Program Overview.png` — "Program Overview" Section
**Where it's used:** Background behind program structure cards (6 Weeks, Open Cohorts, 2–3 Hours/Week, Project-Based) and experience cards (Communication, Public Speaking, Final Showcase).

**Whisk prompt:**
> Wide landscape illustration showing a 6-week journey timeline as a visual path or road, with small illustrated scenes along the way — a student writing an email, a student holding a microphone, a student presenting to a crowd. Warm orange and golden yellow color palette. Birds-eye or slightly elevated perspective. The path should curve through the image from left to right. Illustrated, slightly whimsical. Top area should be lighter/more open for text.

---

### 5. `Meet Your Mentors.png` — "Meet Your Mentors" Section
**Where it's used:** Background behind the "Meet Your Mentors" heading, subtitle, and a call-to-action button.

**Whisk prompt:**
> Wide landscape illustration of friendly high school students (ages 16–18) in a warm, welcoming setting — sitting at desks or standing casually, smiling, some with laptops open. Diverse group — different ethnicities, styles. Warm golden light, cream and orange tones. They look like real students, not corporate or stock-photo. The center and top of the image should be slightly softer/lighter for text overlay. Illustrated style with gentle textures.

---

## Decorative / Side Images

These appear as semi-transparent decorative elements (30–50% opacity), positioned to the right side of a section. They should have **transparent or very clean backgrounds**.

---

### 6. `Mascot.png` — CTA Section ("Ready to Find Your Fire?")
**Where it's used:** Right side of the big orange CTA box at the bottom of the home page. Semi-transparent (30% opacity). Visible on desktop only.

**Whisk prompt:**
> A friendly illustrated flame character or mascot — stylized fire shape with a cute expressive face (big eyes, small smile). Orange and yellow gradient body, warm glow around it. Transparent background (PNG). Facing slightly left toward center. Tall portrait orientation. Fun and approachable — like a friendly school mascot. Not scary or intense. Could be holding a small diploma or star.

---

### 7. `Graduation.png` — Apply Page Header
**Where it's used:** Right side of the orange "Apply to Ignite" page header. Semi-transparent (20–25% opacity).

**Whisk prompt:**
> An illustrated graduation cap (mortarboard) with a golden tassel, floating with small sparkle stars and confetti around it. Transparent background (PNG). Warm golden and orange tones. Clean, simple graphic design style. Portrait orientation. Celebratory and optimistic mood.

---

## Icon Images

These are small square icons used inside cards and activity sections. **Transparent PNG background, portrait or square orientation.**

---

### 8. `Laptop.png` — Professional Communication Icon
**Where it's used:** "Professional Communication" cards across Home, Curriculum, and Program pages.

**Whisk prompt:**
> Illustrated laptop with a glowing warm orange screen showing a simple email or document icon. Clean, flat illustration style. Transparent background. Square composition. Friendly rounded corners on the laptop. Golden warm color palette.

---

### 9. `Microphone.png` — Public Speaking Icon
**Where it's used:** "Public Speaking" cards across Home, Curriculum, and Program pages.

**Whisk prompt:**
> Illustrated handheld microphone with warm orange and golden tones. Simple, clean flat illustration. A few small sound wave lines coming from the side. Transparent background. Square composition. Friendly and energetic feel.

---

### 10. `Trophy.png` — Final Showcase / Achievement Icon
**Where it's used:** "Final Showcase" cards and Week 6 Shark Tank sections.

**Whisk prompt:**
> Illustrated golden trophy cup with orange and yellow gradient shine. Small star sparks floating around it. Clean flat illustration style. Transparent background. Square composition. Celebratory and exciting vibe.

---

### 11. `Artist.png` — Visual Design / Branding Icon
**Where it's used:** "Visual Design" and "Digital Branding" cards across Curriculum and Program pages.

**Whisk prompt:**
> Illustrated artist palette and paintbrush with warm orange, yellow, and cream color blobs on the palette. Clean flat illustration style. Transparent background. Square composition. Creative and playful energy.

---

### 12. `Lightbulb.png` — Career Exploration / Empowerment Icon
**Where it's used:** "Career Exploration" and "Empowerment" cards on About and Program pages.

**Whisk prompt:**
> Illustrated lightbulb glowing with warm golden-orange light. Small sparkle stars around the glow. Simple flat illustration style. Transparent background. Square composition. The bulb itself should be glass-clear with a warm orange filament glowing inside.

---

### 13. `Mentors.png` — Open Cohorts / Mentorship Icon
**Where it's used:** "Open Cohorts" program structure card on Program page.

**Whisk prompt:**
> Illustrated group of three small friendly people silhouettes in a circle — representing a cohort or team. Warm orange and cream tones. Simple flat illustration style. Transparent background. Square composition. Inclusive and welcoming feel — diverse shapes/hairstyles.

---

### 14. `Logo.png` — Brand Logo (Navbar & Footer)
**Where it's used:** Top-left of the navbar and footer, shown at 32–40px square.

**Whisk prompt:**
> A small square logo mark for "Ignite" — a stylized flame or spark symbol in warm orange (#F07B2A) and golden yellow (#FFD166). Clean, bold, minimal — must be readable at 32px. Transparent background. No text, just the icon mark. The flame should feel energetic and upward-moving.

---

### 15. `404 Error.png` — 404 Page Illustration
**Where it's used:** Center of the 404 Not Found page, with bounce animation.

**Whisk prompt:**
> A friendly illustrated "404" with a confused or lost flame mascot character next to the numbers. Warm orange and cream color palette. The flame character looks puzzled — question marks floating around it. Transparent background. Landscape or square orientation. Clean illustrated style, slightly playful and humorous.
