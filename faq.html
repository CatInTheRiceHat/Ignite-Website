FAQ 🔥

Got questions? We've got answers.

💸 Is it free?

Yes! Ignite is 100% free. No tuition, no materials fees, no hidden costs. We believe every student deserves access to mentorship regardless of budget.

📅 How long is the program?

6 weeks during the summer. We design activities to fit around family vacations and other summer commitments.

⏱️ What's the time commitment?

About 2-3 hours per week, including live sessions and independent projects. We keep it manageable so students can enjoy their summer.

📚 Is experience required?

Not at all! We welcome all students regardless of prior skills or grades. Just bring curiosity and willingness to learn.

👥 Who are the mentors?

High school students from local schools who have been trained to mentor. They're relatable, approachable, and genuinely care about helping you succeed.

🎓 Who can apply?

Any student currently in grades 6-8. We especially encourage underrepresented students to apply, but all curious minds are welcome.

📍 Where is it held?

The program is based in Saratoga, CA. Exact location and format (in-person/virtual) will be shared with accepted students.

📋 How do I get accepted?

Fill out the application form! We review each application personally and accept students on a rolling basis. Spots are limited, so apply early.

🏆 What do students get at the end?

Students receive a certificate of completion, a portfolio of work they can showcase, mentor connections, and most importantly — new skills and confidence.

📧 How do I contact you?

Email us at ignite.saratoga@gmail.com with any questions not answered here. We typically respond within 24-48 hours.

Still Have Questions?

We're here to help! Reach out and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
