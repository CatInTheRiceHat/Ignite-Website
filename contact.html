For Parents

We know you have questions. Here's what you need to know.

✅ Is this legitimate?

Yes! Ignite is a student-led nonprofit initiative. Our mentors are vetted high school students passionate about education equity.

🔒 Is my child safe?

Absolutely. All mentors are trained in youth safety guidelines. Parents are welcome to observe sessions.

💰 Are there any costs?

None. The program is 100% free. We're funded by community support and believe in access for all.

📧 How do I stay informed?

We send weekly updates to parents with what students are learning and any important announcements.
